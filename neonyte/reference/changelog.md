# Changelog

## Version 1.0

<p style="font-size:0.75rem; font-weight:400; opacity:0.38; letter-spacing:0.02em; margin:-0.85rem 0 1.2rem;">9 September 2026</p>

- New: procedural neon signs from placeholder boxes, one sign each.
- New: viewport placement tool for placing, editing and erasing signs.
- New: content engine picks type, name, icon and colours together, in five scripts.
- New: 82 layout templates plus a procedural layout generator, with 46 decor kinds.
- New: three-level procedural hardware, with standoffs that reach the building and rooftop trusses.
- New: Karma CPU and XPU setup from one button, with mesh-light and flicker options.
- New: Adjustment Notes for steering the street in plain English.
- New: per-sign cards with re-roll, title lock, business groups and a per-sign note.
- New: 52 Manual Override rows that pin any decision across the street.
- New: two VEXpression hooks, before and after the plan.
- New: Deterioration and Flicker, with per-sign amounts.
- New: LOD presets stepping tube detail down for distant signs.
- New: every font OFL and every icon ISC or MIT, licences embedded.
