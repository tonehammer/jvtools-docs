# Changelog

## Version 1.0.1
<p style="font-size:0.75rem; font-weight:400; opacity:0.38; letter-spacing:0.02em; margin:-0.85rem 0 1.2rem;">20 October 2026</p>

* New: **slidersFollowClip** on the Face gear moves the sliders on the face with the clip.
* **Fixed:** two-shape face sliders drove their first shape at double strength.
* **Fixed:** the APEX Rig's type properties named a development build.

## Version 1.0
<p style="font-size:0.75rem; font-weight:400; opacity:0.38; letter-spacing:0.02em; margin:-0.85rem 0 1.2rem;">12 October 2026</p>

The first release.

**Import and look development**

* Character Creator 5 and iClone 8 characters, FBX or USD, with Karma XPU MaterialX materials and the lookdev controller.

**Rig**

* New: APEX control rig, built by **Build Character**.
* New: facial rig with every morph channel keyable, on-face sliders and a face board.
* New: **Character Picker** pages for body, hands, feet and face.

**Animation**

* New: **Face Puppet** recording with Character Creator's puppet profiles and Full Face presets.
* New: **Face Key Muscle** posing on any layer, with Ease In / Ease Out and recording over time.
* New: 301 expression presets and 71 hand poses in the Animation Catalog.
* New: **Auto Blink** and **Auto Breathe**.
* New: **Look At** with gaze-driven eye morphs.
* New: motion clips with **Play Clip**, and layer tools including **Revert to Clip**.
* New: **Stash Rig** for fast reopening of saved scenes.
