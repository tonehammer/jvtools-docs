## Version 1.0

The first public release.

**Output window**

* Borderless, chrome-free output window for a Copernicus network, on any screen.
* Fullscreen, or a window at the COP's own resolution.
* Always On Top, and Hide Cursor over the output.
* Auto-Open On Load, for installations that come back up by themselves.
* Mirrors the playbar — no second clock.

**Source**

* Three sources — the COP image, a clean 3D viewport, or the viewport over the COP.
* Max FPS cap, and a Status readout of the measured read time and implied frame rate.
* Built-in test pattern with preset aspects, working with nothing wired in.

**Placement**

* Fit modes — letterbox, crop, 1:1 pixels and stretch.
* Offset, zoom and horizontal/vertical flips, all saved with the scene.
* Right-drag, wheel, arrow-key and keyboard control of the window.
* Smooth Scaling toggle, and a Reset Position button.

**Output grade**

* Exposure and Gamma Trim, applied on the GPU before display.

**Utilities**

* Blackout — instant cut to black without closing the window.
* Show Diagnostics overlay on the output window.
* Quick links to documentation and community.
